# 19. Second Majors and Minors

Menu: **Students and staff > Second major and minor applications**, and the **Second majors and minors** tab of the student record detail

Students apply in the portal and the staff of the program applied for decide. On approval the second major or minor is added to the student record. The student record (student number) stays the same.

## Preparation

1. Under **Reference data > Programs** turn on **Accepts second majors** and **Accepts minors** for the programs that take them.
2. Under **Settings > Student records** set how many second majors and minors a student may have at a time (default 1 each).
3. Create a **Second major and minor applications** window among the **term windows** of the term in which applications are taken. The target criteria can limit it to certain year levels (e.g. years 2 and 3). An approved program is followed from the first day of that term.
4. Create the second major and minor requirements of the program under **Curriculum and sections > Graduation requirements** ([chapter 17](17-courses-and-curriculum.md)).

## Student application

During the application window a student applies in the portal under **My records**, entering the kind (second major, minor), the program and the reason. The application can be cancelled until it is decided.

- Only enrolled or on-leave student records can apply.
- A student cannot apply for their own primary program or a program they already follow.
- The number of programs followed plus the number of pending applications cannot exceed the limit.
- Only one pending application per program.

## Approval

In the **Second major and minor applications** list (default: pending) click **Approve** or **Reject** (a note is required).

- The `Decide second major and minor applications` permission must have the program applied for (or its org unit) in its scope. Example: a department assistant of the Korean Language and Literature department assigned with that department's scope decides only that department's applications.
- The deciding staff member can see the student record of the applicant. Staff of the student's primary department see their students' applications but cannot decide them.
- Nobody can decide an application they made themselves.
- After approval, the staff of that program's org unit (student record viewing scope) can see this student's record. Section eligibility criteria also treat the student as a student of that program.

## Ending

In the **Second majors and minors** tab of the student record detail click **End** and enter the end date and the reason. This can be done by the registrar (student record scope) or by the deciding staff of that program. A program that was ended can be applied for again later.
